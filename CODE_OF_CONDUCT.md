# Code of Conduct

This repository is a public product feedback space for Vivify.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific and constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on the product, documentation, or user workflow.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Avoid posting private data, secrets, or content you do not have permission to share.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or abusive language
- Spam or unrelated promotion
- Sharing credentials, personal data, or private customer information
- Posting copyrighted or private generated assets without permission
- Attempts to misuse this repository as a support channel for sensitive account issues

Maintainers may edit, close, hide, or remove content that does not fit this public feedback space.
