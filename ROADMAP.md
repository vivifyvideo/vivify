# Vivify Public Roadmap

This roadmap summarizes product directions we are evaluating or actively improving for [Vivify](https://vivify.video). It covers image generation, image editing, text-to-video, image-to-video, and realtime face swap. It is not a promise of delivery dates, pricing, model access, or final feature scope.

## Now

- Keep image and video creation paths clear while preserving fast movement between generation and editing workflows.
- Make model capabilities, reference limits, and credit costs easier to compare before generation.
- Improve prompt-backed examples that move users from showcase browsing into creation.
- Improve realtime face-swap setup, session status, and recovery guidance.
- Keep account history, billing, and credit-pack flows understandable across creative workflows.

## Next

- Expand guidance for image generation and reference-based edits that become video source material.
- Improve failed-generation explanations and retry paths without hiding provider limits.
- Add clearer examples for product, social, cinematic, and motion-design use cases.
- Make realtime face-swap requirements and live-session states easier to understand.
- Refine localization for users comparing image and video workflows in English and Chinese.

## Later

- Expose more structured recipes across image, video, and realtime workflows.
- Evaluate deeper media organization for teams producing many variations.
- Improve cross-model education when new provider capabilities become available.
- Document public examples that show practical tradeoffs instead of generic model hype.

## Product Principles

- Present image generation, image editing, video generation, and realtime face swap as connected product capabilities.
- Keep workflow guidance concrete enough to help users choose the right path.
- Credit and capability claims should stay tied to the live product state.
- This repository is for public product feedback, not private production source code.
